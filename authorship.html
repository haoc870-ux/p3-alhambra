The story behind the symbol

A familiar symbol of luck, shaped by a shared creative culture. Alhambra is not officially attributed to one named designer. Van Cleef & Arpels presents it as a creation of the Maison, shaped by a shared language of symbols, craftsmanship, and tradition.

A shared belief in luck

Jacques Arpels helped give that language its emotional meaning by offering four-leaf clovers as lucky charms. A small natural form became a gesture of optimism: something to carry, to offer, and to believe in.

A shared authorship

At 22 Place Vendôme, the house’s home since 1906, that symbol became part of a larger cultural memory and eventually a consistent design system. The collection grew from shared beliefs, repeated decisions, and an enduring language of craftsmanship. Its identity belongs to that continuity: a familiar form carried forward through the hands of the Maison’s artisans.
